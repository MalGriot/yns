## 02 · Logo & Symbol

The primary mark is **YNS** in iridescent, chameleon-shift color, with **YASS** and **SASS** locked underneath. It only ever appears on black or bone — never on a busy photo without a solid plate behind it.

**Lockups**
- **Primary lockup** — iridescent, always shimmering, YNS on black, full wordmark in white directly beneath, always centered, never justified wide.
- **Reversed lockup** — solid ink YNS on bone, for light applications like print or packaging tags, where the shimmer can't render.
- **Glow is allowed** — a soft neon bloom behind the mark on dark digital surfaces (loading states, hero moments) is on-brand. Keep it soft; the mark should glow like a light source, not wear a sticker outline.

**Don'ts**
- Never recolor the gradient to a flat brand color, stretch the mark, or set it on a mid-tone background where it loses contrast.

**Peacock feather symbol** — the brand's second mark is a real peacock feather, shot not drawn: a single feather, eye and all, photographed against black. No illustration earns the iridescence honestly, so the mark is always sourced from a real photograph, never a redrawn or vectorized version. It's the literal source of the brand's color logic — the same shift-with-the-light shimmer that runs through the wordmark and the palette. Use it standalone as an avatar, stamp, or hangtag seal, always on black.

## 03 · Color & Iridescence

Black is the stage — but the peacock's chest is the whole point. Iridescence isn't a rare accent here, it's the brand's core material: it moves, it catches light, it shows up everywhere color does. Chrome is what makes that possible — the metallic base every shimmer is built on.

| Name | Hex | Shimmers toward |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#0B0C10` | — (flat neutral) |
| Bone | `#F3EFE6` | — (flat neutral) |
| Chrome | `#9AA0AC` | dark steel → bright specular hit → dark steel |
| Peacock Teal | `#16A39A` | cyan `#2BE8D4` → blue |
| Peacock Blue | `#3F7FD6` | sky `#6BA0FF` → violet |
| Peacock Magenta | `#C4419A` | pink `#FF5FCE` → violet |
| Peacock Gold | `#D9A13C` | warm gold `#FFCF5C` → magenta |

**Chrome is the odd one that isn't a hue at all** — a dark-to-bright-to-dark metallic sweep, blended underneath every shimmer (wordmark, lockup, the signature gradient strip) the same way a real feather's color only shows because the structure underneath is catching light like polished metal. It's the physical shine the color rides on top of, not a color itself.

**Every named hue is a shimmer, not a chip** — teal drifts toward cyan and blue, magenta drifts toward violet and pink, gold drifts toward magenta. Nothing in this palette is meant to sit still. Only Ink and Bone stay flat; they're the neutral stage the shimmer performs on.

**Trip. Glow. Repeat.** Ravers chase the glow — so key moments (the wordmark, the feather, a hover state, a drop countdown) get a soft neon bloom behind them. Glow marks a moment; it doesn't fill a room.

**Motion moves on scroll, not on a timer.** The shimmer sweep and glow intensity are driven by scroll position, not an autoplaying loop — the identity only comes alive when the reader moves through the page. It holds still the instant they stop.

**Usage split:** Ink 40% · Bone 18% · Iridescence 24% · Chrome 11% · Glow 7%

## 04 · Type System

A poster-condensed display face for shout-lines, a warm humanist sans for everything you actually read, and a mono face for specs, drops and prices.

| Role | Typeface | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Display | Anton | Headlines, genre names, campaign lines |
| Body | Manrope (Regular / Medium / Bold) | Product copy, editorial, UI |
| Mono | JetBrains Mono | Prices, SKUs, timestamps, labels |

> Example headline: *Dress For The Night You're Having*
> Example mono label: `DROP_04 · BOHO-PSY-DRESS-M · ₹3,200 · RESTOCK 21.08`

## 06 · Voice

She's the friend who gets you dressed and gets you out the door on time. Direct, playful, a little bit dared — never apologetic, never precious.

**We sound like**
- A dare, not a suggestion. *"Wear the thing you almost didn't buy."*
- Genre-fluent. We know the difference between a Boho Psy crowd and a Berlin warehouse.
- Confident and short. One good line beats three hedged ones.
- In on the culture. PLURP, omY'S Club and drop language used like they're already common knowledge.

**We don't sound like**
- A fast-fashion blast. No "HUGE SALE!!!" energy or discount-first messaging.
- Traditional "ethnic wear" marketing. We're Indian-born, not a heritage costume brand.
- A Western festival brand in translation. No borrowed Coachella clichés.
- Corporate or apologetic. Never hedge a bold look with "if you dare" disclaimers.

**Sample lines**

| Context | Line |
|---|---|
| Drop announcement | The Berlin Underground drop is live. Dress like the bassline. |
| Product page | Built for a night that starts at 11 and doesn't check the time again. |
| Community / omY'S Club | You don't shop here. You belong here. |

## 07 · Beyond Fashion

**Rural craft. Urban night.**

Supporting rural women of India to help urban women of India party better. Where possible, the ecosystem creates opportunities through craftsmanship, embellishment, textiles and production — connecting Indian craft with India's nightlife culture. Not framed as charity: as part of the value chain, from making to designing to wearing.

Fashion is the entry point. The long-term platform extends into:

- Jewellery, bags, footwear & body adornment
- Beauty and nightlife essentials
- Limited artist & DJ collaborations
- Festival pop-ups and brand experiences
- omY'S Club membership and party updates
